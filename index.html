Course Synthesis

This portfolio is the final requirement for EGE 312: People and the Earth's Ecosystem. Throughout this course, we examined the delicate balance between human progress and environmental sustainability. From the importance of urban tree canopies to the devastating impacts of irresponsible mining, this collection documents our team's active engagement with nature and our proposed solutions for a greener, more sustainable future.
