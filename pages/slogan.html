Activity III: Environmental Awareness

Catchy slogans and deep-dive essays addressing pressing local issues like climate change, mining, and water pollution.

Stellar

🌍 Topic: Mining

"Gold in the soil, poison in the stream; wake up from the mining dream!"

The Literal Meaning: On a literal level, this slogan addresses the physical process and immediate consequences of irresponsible mineral extraction. It highlights that while operations dig into the earth to find valuable resources like gold, the runoff and chemical byproducts—such as mercury and cyanide—inevitably find their way into adjacent waterways, poisoning the rivers and streams.

The Symbolism: Symbolically, the slogan serves as a critique of short-term economic greed. The "mining dream" represents the illusion that resource extraction brings unchecked wealth and progress to a region without consequences. The juxtaposition of "gold" and "poison" emphasizes that the temporary wealth of a few results in the permanent ecological poverty of the many.

Local Relevance: I chose this topic because mining is a pressing issue in the Davao Region, particularly in areas like Pantukan and the wider Davao de Oro province. The promise of economic upliftment often blinds people to the realities of deforestation, landslides, and heavy metal contamination in the water table. As a resident of Davao, the health of our regional watersheds is directly tied to our future survival, making it imperative to push back against destructive extraction practices.
