Activity I: Connecting with Nature

A compilation of our team's observations and reflections while spending intentional time with trees in our local communities.

Stellar

🌿 The Narra at USeP Obrero

The Observation: For this activity, I chose a mature Narra tree located near the USeP Obrero campus grounds. Observing its structure, I noticed the massive, deeply grooved bark that felt rough and slightly damp to the touch, harboring small colonies of moss near the roots. The canopy was expansive, filtering the harsh afternoon sun into dappled light. Around its base, the soil was compacted from foot traffic, yet the tree stood resilient, with roots visibly anchoring it deep into the earth. It was surrounded by a mix of fallen dry leaves and small insects navigating the crevices of its trunk.

The Reflection "Hugging the tree and pausing for a few minutes made me feel surprisingly grounded, providing a stark, quiet contrast to the fast-paced environment of a CS student. It was a moment of stillness that made me realize how alive these structures are. Trees are indispensable to the Earth's ecosystem; they act as primary carbon sinks, purifying the air we breathe, while their root systems prevent soil erosion—a crucial function for flood-prone areas in Davao. They are silent sentinels that sustain the environmental balance humans rely on daily."
